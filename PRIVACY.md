## 简体中文

**QQPlayer 隐私政策**

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 30 日

QQPlayer（"本应用"）是一款本地音乐播放器。我们致力于保护你的隐私：**本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人身份信息**。

**1. 数据收集**

本应用不收集任何个人数据：
- 无账号系统，不要求注册或登录
- 无广告 SDK、无统计分析、无第三方追踪
- 不访问你的通讯录、位置、照片或麦克风（Siri 语音控制由 iOS 系统处理，不经过本应用服务器）

**2. 音乐文件**

- 你的音乐文件仅存储在设备本地或你自己的 iCloud Drive 中
- 本应用不会将你的音乐文件上传到任何服务器
- iCloud 同步由 Apple 的 iCloud 服务提供，适用 Apple 的隐私政策

**3. 歌词服务**

当你播放歌曲并请求歌词时，本应用会向第三方歌词服务（网易云音乐、LRCLIB）发送**歌曲标题和歌手名**，仅用于匹配歌词文本。这些服务有各自的隐私政策，本应用无法控制其数据处理方式。歌词搜索结果仅保存在你的设备上。

**4. 本地数据**

本应用在设备本地保存以下数据，仅用于功能实现，不会离开你的设备：
- 播放历史与播放统计（用于生成智能歌单）
- 你手动指定的歌词、EQ 设置、界面偏好

**5. 第三方服务**

- iCloud Drive：Apple Inc.，见 https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- 网易云音乐歌词：https://music.163.com/
- LRCLIB：https://lrclib.net/

**6. 儿童隐私**

本应用面向全年龄段，不收集任何用户的个人信息，包括儿童。

**7. 联系方式**

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系：https://github.com/daxmate/qqplayer-ios/issues

**8. 政策变更**

如本政策发生变更，我们会在本页面更新并修订"生效日期"。

## English

**QQPlayer Privacy Policy**

Effective date: August 30, 2026

QQPlayer ("the App") is a local music player. We are committed to protecting your privacy: **the App does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information.**

**1. Data Collection**

The App collects no personal data:
- No account system; no registration or login required
- No ad SDKs, no analytics, no third-party tracking
- No access to your contacts, location, photos, or microphone (Siri voice control is handled by iOS itself and never passes through our servers)

**2. Music Files**

- Your music files are stored only on your device or in your own iCloud Drive
- The App never uploads your music files to any server
- iCloud sync is provided by Apple's iCloud service and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy

**3. Lyrics Services**

When you play a song and request lyrics, the App sends the **song title and artist name** to third-party lyrics services (NetEase Music, LRCLIB) solely to match lyric text. These services have their own privacy policies, and we cannot control how they process data. Lyrics search results are stored only on your device.

**4. Local Data**

The App stores the following data locally on your device, used only for features and never leaving your device:
- Play history and play statistics (used to generate smart playlists)
- Manually assigned lyrics, EQ settings, interface preferences

**5. Third-Party Services**

- iCloud Drive: Apple Inc., https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- NetEase Music lyrics: https://music.163.com/
- LRCLIB: https://lrclib.net/

**6. Children's Privacy**

The App is designed for all ages and collects no personal information from any user, including children.

**7. Contact**

For privacy questions, please contact us via GitHub Issues: https://github.com/daxmate/qqplayer-ios/issues

**8. Policy Changes**

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Effective date" above.
